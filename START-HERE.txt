OUR LITTLE FOREVER ♡
RANDOM MOMENTS EDITION — NO CALENDAR, NO DATES FOR PHOTOS

1. Extract ZIP.
2. Replace the old index.html, styles.css, app.js and data.js on GitHub with the new ones.
3. Also keep favicon.svg, little-stars.wav, .nojekyll in your GitHub repository root.
4. GitHub Settings > Pages: deploy from branch main, root.
5. Visit your Pages link and hard-refresh after the deployment.

OPTIONAL PHOTOS: put photo-01.jpg, photo-02.jpg, ... photo-40.jpg under photos/.
No dates or memory matching required. Photos work in random order.
Do not just rename .png to .jpg — convert to JPEG first.

For detailed help, read README.md.
